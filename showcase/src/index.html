ABOUT THIS DEMO

About the demo

Try the built-in classifier, change a category, or complete a task. Everything runs in this tab. Reloading clears your notes and labels.

No account, analytics or inference API. The page loads static files from its host; the host may keep normal request logs.

This is a browser demo. Android adds MiniLM, personal classifier training, encrypted storage, files and media. Limits here: 2,000 characters per note, 20 notes and 100 saved labels.
